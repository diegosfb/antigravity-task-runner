# Business impact input schema

Use this only when the conversation does not already provide enough inputs.

## Business goals

- Target outcome and current pain
- Measurement period and decision deadline
- Constraints or conservative guardrails

## Proposed artifacts

- Feature, workflow, or deliverable
- Users and business process affected
- Expected behavior or process change

## Client baselines

| Lever | Useful baselines |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Qualified volume, current conversion, expected lift, revenue per conversion, period |
| Efficiency | Current task time, expected time saved, workers affected, fully loaded hourly cost, realizable capacity |
| Retention | Customer base, churn period/rate, expected reduction, annual revenue per customer |
| Risk | Annual incidents, cost per incident, expected reduction, control effectiveness |

Label source, date, unit, and confidence for every baseline. Distinguish user-provided facts from assumptions.
