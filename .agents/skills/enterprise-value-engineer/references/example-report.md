# Worked example: self-service onboarding

## Strategic Fit

A self-service onboarding portal primarily reduces manual onboarding work and may secondarily improve retention through faster time to value.

## Mapping

| Artifact | Primary lever | Mechanism | Quantifiable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-service workflow | Efficiency | Reduces recurring handling time | Yes |
| Automated verification | Risk | Reduces manual errors and incidents | Yes |
| Status tracking | Retention | Reduces uncertainty during onboarding | Not without evidence |

## Primary calculation

Assume 30 total team hours saved weekly, a $45 fully loaded hourly cost, and 70% realizable capacity:

`30 × $45 × 52 × 70% = $49,140 annual capacity value`

This is not cash savings unless staffing or external spend is avoided; otherwise it is capacity available for higher-value work.

## Confidence

`Medium`: labor inputs are known, but realization and retention effects require post-launch measurement.
