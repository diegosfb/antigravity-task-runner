# Value-lever mapping

Choose the closest direct financial mechanism, not the most attractive narrative.

| Lever | Use when the primary outcome is | Typical artifacts |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | More qualified purchases, expansion, or revenue per transaction | Checkout, pricing, lead routing, recommendations, sales enablement |
| Efficiency | Less recurring labor, handling, review, or support demand | Workflow automation, admin tools, reporting, document generation |
| Risk | Lower incident frequency or financial consequence | Controls, audit trails, fraud prevention, recovery, approval workflows |
| Retention | Less churn through improved adoption, reliability, service, or time to value | Onboarding, self-service, reliability, support, account transparency |

Most artifacts have secondary effects. Assign one primary lever, note the others, and quantify a secondary effect only when it has an independent baseline. Never add two calculations that represent the same customer or operational outcome.
