# Financial formulas

Show inputs, units, period conversions, calculation, annual result, source, and uncertainty.

## Efficiency

`Annual capacity value = hours saved per worker per week × workers affected × fully loaded hourly cost × 52 × realization rate`

Use the lower credible time-saving bound. Include a realization rate because freed capacity is value only when redeployed or avoided. Do not describe capacity value as cash savings unless spend actually falls.

## Revenue

`Annual revenue impact = qualified volume per period × percentage-point conversion lift × revenue per conversion × periods per year`

Use qualified—not total—volume. Separate revenue from profit and state whether the amount is ARR, bookings, recognized revenue, or gross profit.

## Retention

`Annual retained revenue = customer base × percentage-point churn reduction × annual revenue per customer`

For monthly churn, use `annual churn = 1 − (1 − monthly churn)^12`; do not multiply monthly churn by 12. Avoid adding retained revenue to another calculation based on the same customers.

## Risk

`Annual expected-loss reduction = baseline annual incidents × expected reduction × average loss per incident`

Expected loss is probability-weighted, not guaranteed cash. Include control adoption and effectiveness where material. Use external incident-cost benchmarks only after verifying and citing them.

## Confidence

- `High`: current client baselines and a direct, evidenced causal link.
- `Medium`: one to three material assumptions or moderate causal uncertainty.
- `Low`: four or more material assumptions, stale inputs, or weak causal evidence.

Use scenario ranges instead of a single number when input uncertainty would materially alter the decision.
