# Example output pattern

### Verdict
The insurer appears to have processed the claim correctly, but the negotiated hospital rate is high relative to local freestanding alternatives and is worth asking to reduce to the facility's published cash price.

### Claim math
| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Billed | $600 |
| Contractual discount | -$300 |
| Allowed | $300 |
| Insurer paid | $0 |
| Patient responsibility | $300 |

### Price benchmark
| Benchmark | Amount | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Facility cash price | $280 | Strongest negotiation anchor |
| Local independent imaging | $90-$140 | Shows hospital premium |
| Medicare | $40 | Reference floor only |

### What I would do
Ask billing for a courtesy adjustment to the current cash price first. If denied, request an itemized bill and confirm no separate professional charge is pending.
