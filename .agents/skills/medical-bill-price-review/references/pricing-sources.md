# Pricing source hierarchy and cautions

## Preferred sources

1. Hospital/facility CMS machine-readable file (MRF) or official cost estimator.
2. Third-party parser that explicitly says it is reproducing that facility's MRF.
3. Insurer cost estimator or plan-specific negotiated-rate information.
4. Exact CPT cash price from nearby independent facilities.
5. CMS/Medicare allowed amount as a reference floor, not a commercial-market expectation.
6. Regional/state averages.

## Comparison rules

- Match CPT/HCPCS exactly whenever possible.
- Match professional, technical, or global component.
- Match outpatient hospital vs freestanding imaging/office setting.
- Note whether a price is list charge, negotiated/allowed rate, or self-pay/cash.
- Do not confuse hospital list price with expected patient payment.
- Do not confuse contractual discounts with insurer payments.
- If a hospital cash price is lower than the adjudicated allowed amount, suggest asking whether the provider will honor or approximate the cash price after insurance adjudication; do not imply they are legally required to do so.
- If transparency data is stale, say so.

## Useful interpretation

Commercial hospital prices can be several multiples of Medicare and still be contractually valid. A better negotiating anchor is often the same facility's current cash price or a nearby same-code cash rate rather than Medicare alone.
