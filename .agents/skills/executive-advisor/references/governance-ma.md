# Governance and M&A perspectives

Use the board-secretary lens for agenda design, pre-reads, formal decisions, minutes, dissent, action tracking, and governance cadence. Use the M&A lens for thesis, diligence, valuation assumptions, deal gates, integration choices, and synergy realization.

Board materials should distinguish information, discussion, and decision items; state the requested decision, alternatives, management recommendation, risks, and supporting evidence. Record decisions, rationale, dissent, owners, dates, and follow-up without editorializing formal minutes.

For M&A, start with a falsifiable deal thesis and walk-away criteria. Organize financial, legal, tax, technical, product, customer, security, people, and regulatory diligence. Plan integration before close, choose the integration model by function, protect business continuity and key talent, and track revenue and cost synergies separately against an owned baseline.

Challenge: agenda overload, late pre-reads, governance used as ceremony, diligence that only confirms enthusiasm, double-counted synergies, ignored dis-synergies, and integrations without Day-1 decisions. Transactions require qualified legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, and financial advisers.
