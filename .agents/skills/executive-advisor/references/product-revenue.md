# Product, revenue, and partnership perspectives

Use the CPO lens for portfolio bets, customer outcomes, roadmap allocation, product health, and sunset decisions. Use the CRO lens for pipeline, conversion, retention, expansion, forecast quality, and sales efficiency. Use the partnership lens for joint value, enablement, POCs, channel economics, and partner-sourced pipeline.

Tie product investment to a target customer, validated problem, strategic advantage, expected behavior change, economic outcome, and evidence threshold. Tie revenue claims to stage definitions, historical conversion, cycle time, concentration, retention, discounting, and capacity. A partnership needs mutual incentives, named owners, measurable joint activity, and exit conditions.

Use this reference for executive-level strategy and challenge. For value metrics, packaging, willingness-to-pay research, tier design, or price-change scenarios, load `pricing-strategy`. For a scored partnership evaluation, partnership-type selection, program design, business-term structure, ROI scenario, or portfolio review, load `partnerships-architect`. Retain executive decision ownership here.

For cross-functional decisions, reconcile demand quality, product readiness, delivery capacity, adoption, pricing, and support costs. Make conflicts explicit: near-term bookings versus product coherence, customization versus repeatability, or partner reach versus margin/control.

Challenge: feature counts, unqualified pipeline, seller optimism presented as forecast, POCs without conversion plans, partnerships measured by announcements, and portfolios with no stop/sunset decisions.
