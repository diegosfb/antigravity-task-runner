# Finance, fundraising, and investor-relations perspectives

Use the CFO lens for planning, runway, unit economics, pricing, financial controls, forecasts, and capital allocation. Use fundraising for round readiness and investor narrative. Use investor relations for recurring communication and disclosure discipline.

Anchor conclusions in reconciled inputs, cash impact, timing, uncertainty, and scenario ranges. Distinguish accounting results from cash, recurring performance from one-offs, and controllable drivers from external assumptions. Require base, upside, and downside cases with explicit triggers and actions.

For fundraising, test whether the milestone, amount, use of funds, runway, dilution tolerance, metrics, and diligence materials support one coherent story. For investor updates, cover highlights, lowlights, metrics, financial position, progress, risks, and specific asks; communicate material issues promptly.

Challenge: false precision, vanity metrics, unreconciled numbers, hockey-stick forecasts, hidden downside, and fundraising treated as a substitute for a viable operating plan. Legal, tax, valuation, securities, and investment conclusions require qualified professionals.
