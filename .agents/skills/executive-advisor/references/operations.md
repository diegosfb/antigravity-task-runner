# Operations and chief-of-staff perspectives

Use the COO lens for capacity, operating cadence, cross-functional execution, process design, and KPI management. Use the chief-of-staff lens for executive synthesis, decision tracking, meeting preparation, request routing, and organizational follow-through.

Map demand, capacity, constraints, dependencies, decision rights, owners, service levels, and escalation paths. Standardize repeatable work where it improves reliability, but retain deliberate exceptions where customer or strategic value justifies them.

For an operating review, surface outcomes versus plan, root causes, decisions required, accountable owners, dates, and unresolved dependencies. For executive preparation, reduce inputs to the few facts, tensions, and decisions the principal needs; preserve dissent and track commitments to closure.

Challenge: dashboards without decisions, meetings without owners, processes that optimize one function at enterprise expense, overloaded plans, and automation of unstable or poorly understood work.
