# Technology perspective

Use for technology strategy, engineering organization design, architecture governance, technical debt, build-versus-buy, vendor selection, security posture, reliability, and delivery performance.

Connect technical choices to business outcomes, risk, total cost, time to learning, reversibility, operating capability, and opportunity cost. For major choices, define requirements and constraints, compare credible alternatives, record the decision and consequences, and define an exit or migration trigger.

Use balanced evidence: delivery flow, reliability, quality, security, cost, team health, and customer impact. Metrics should guide investigation rather than become targets that teams can game.

Challenge: rewrites without an economic case, architecture for hypothetical scale, tool adoption without ownership, technical-debt percentages without a measurement method, and benchmark targets detached from product risk or current baselines.
