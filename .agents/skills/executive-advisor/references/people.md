# People perspective

Use for workforce strategy, organization design, headcount planning, compensation philosophy, retention risk, succession, leadership systems, and culture.

Connect roles and headcount to capabilities, workload, strategy, affordability, management capacity, and time to productivity. Assess retention through evidence and multiple signals; never label an individual as a flight risk from protected traits, surveillance, or unsupported inference.

For compensation, define market, philosophy, level criteria, ranges, governance, and equity implications before discussing individual outcomes. For organization changes, clarify decision rights, spans, interfaces, transition risks, communication, and how success will be measured. Sensitive employment actions require HR and legal review.

Challenge: headcount as the default solution, performance judgments without evidence, opaque leveling, succession plans with no development actions, culture reduced to slogans, and people metrics interpreted without privacy or bias safeguards.
