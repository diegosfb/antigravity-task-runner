# User Profile

The user is a **married couple filing jointly (MFJ)** in California with:

- **Income**: $265,000+ per year (above virtually all major phaseout thresholds)
- **Income sources**: W-2 wages, ETF holdings, individual stocks, cryptocurrency, equity compensation (RSUs, ISOs, and/or NSOs)
- **Children**: Two dependents (born 2016 and 2018 — ages 9 and 11 as of 2025 tax year)
- **State**: California (no capital gains preference, no AMT exclusion for ISOs at state level, 13.3% top rate)

**Always proactively check these factors before drawing conclusions:**
- MFJ filing status implications
- High-income phaseouts (Child Tax Credit, education credits, Roth IRA eligibility, deduction limits)
- NIIT exposure ($250,000 MFJ threshold for 3.8% surtax)
- AMT exposure — especially with ISO exercises
- Additional Medicare Tax (0.9% above $250,000 MFJ)
- SALT deduction cap ($10,000)
- CA-specific rules that differ from federal (no QSBS exclusion, no preferential capital gains rates, no ISO AMT relief at state level)

## Clarifying Questions

Ask these when key facts are missing before drawing conclusions on equity or investment questions:

| Topic | Key Missing Info |
|-------|-----------------|
| RSUs | Vesting schedule, current FMV, # of shares, withholding rate, concentration level |
| ISOs | Grant date, exercise price, current FMV, exercise date, # shares, existing AMT credit balance |
| NSOs | Exercise price, FMV, employment vs. consultant status (FICA implications) |
| Capital Gains | Purchase date/price, sale date/price, cost basis method used (FIFO/HIFO/SpecID) |
| Crypto | Lot acquisition dates, cost basis tracking method, exchange records, staking/mining income |
| Retirement | Access to HSA (HDHP enrolled?), 401(k) plan allows after-tax contributions? In-service withdrawals? Roth 401(k) available? Existing pre-tax IRA balances (pro-rata rule) |
| Withholding | W-4 settings, estimated tax payments made YTD, total tax owed prior year |
| AGI | Total AGI estimate for the year (to determine phaseout positions precisely) |
