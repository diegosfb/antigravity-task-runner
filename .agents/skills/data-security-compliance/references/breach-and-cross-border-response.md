# Breach Response and Cross-Border Processing

## Security Incident Boundary

Treat suspected exposure, alteration, loss, or unauthorized access to protected data as a security incident under the repository security procedure. Do not route it as an ordinary data-quality incident.

## Response

1. Stop propagation without destroying evidence.
2. Activate the authorized security, privacy, legal, and communications owners.
3. Preserve safe evidence and establish the affected systems, subjects, records, regions, and time window.
4. Contain access, credentials, sessions, exports, or processing through approved actions.
5. Assess notification and reporting obligations using current law, contracts, regulator guidance, and qualified reviewers.
6. Recover, monitor, communicate, and document decisions and residual exposure.

Never encode notification decisions solely as a severity lookup table. Legal triggers can depend on role, jurisdiction, risk, safeguards, data category, and timing. Track deadlines from the approved incident assessment and preserve the reasoning.

## International Transfers

Inventory origin, destination, controller or processor roles, subprocessors, remote access, support access, storage, backups, and onward transfers. Use only transfer mechanisms and supplementary safeguards approved for the specific flow.

Adequacy decisions, contractual clauses, frameworks, derogations, and local-law assessments are time-sensitive. Verify current authoritative sources and legal approval; do not label distinct mechanisms as interchangeable.

## Technical Safeguards

Consider regional isolation, customer-managed keys, pseudonymization, split control, restricted support access, access logging, data minimization, and contractual processor controls. Document which parties can access plaintext and from which jurisdictions.

## Exercises

Exercise detection, escalation, evidence preservation, decision ownership, regulator and subject communications, processor coordination, credential response, and recovery using synthetic scenarios. Do not place real incident data or sensitive records in training artifacts.
