# Council protocol

This follows the three-stage LLM Council pattern: independent first opinions, anonymous peer review/ranking, then chairman synthesis. Adapt review criteria to story-point calibration rather than generic answer quality.

## Evidence packet
Include the new task plus historical records/anchors. All estimators receive identical evidence. Keep IDs and assigned historical points intact.

## First opinion
Require: proposed Fibonacci points, confidence, up to three real historical analogs, decisive complexity dimensions, and concise rationale. Explicitly prohibit hours/velocity/productivity reasoning.

## Blind review
Replace model names with A/B/C. Ask reviewers to rank proposals by consistency with historical anchors; identify overlooked scope, uncertainty, dependencies, integration/testing burden, and any AI/familiarity-driven point deflation.

## Chairman
Synthesize rather than average. A minority estimate can win when its evidence exposes a material omission. Return only the required four fields. Confidence combines evidence quality and council convergence.
