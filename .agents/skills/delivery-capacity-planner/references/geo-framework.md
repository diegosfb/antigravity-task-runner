# Geo decision framework

For TEAM_DESIGN, score candidate geographies qualitatively unless actual data is supplied.

Consider:
- mandatory client/data/residency constraints
- working-hours overlap and handoff design
- language and stakeholder proximity
- required-skill availability evidence
- ability to form complete pods rather than fragmented individuals
- leadership and onboarding capacity
- concentration and business-continuity risk
- 24x7 or follow-the-sun needs
- travel/on-site requirements
- recruiting lead-time evidence when available
- cost/rate only when supplied or explicitly requested with valid data

Prefer cohesive delivery units. Avoid scattering a small team across many countries merely to optimize theoretical availability.

Explain location recommendations with delivery consequences. If exact supply/cost data is absent, use terms such as higher/lower expected feasibility and mark them benchmark-based rather than factual.
