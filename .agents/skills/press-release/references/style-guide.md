# Press Release Style Guide

The supplied examples use an enterprise-newsroom tone: direct announcement, concrete operational relevance, explanatory context, credible executive perspective, and a practical next step.

## Preserve

- Lead with the organization and action: launched, appointed, partnered, introduced, announced, or recognized.
- Explain the news in business and audience terms before technical detail.
- Use short paragraphs followed by a compact capability or commitment list when it improves scanning.
- Let quotations explain stakes, strategy, or human impact rather than repeat features.
- Connect the announcement to a broader direction only when that direction is supported by supplied facts.
- End with a clear information or participation link, approved boilerplate, and media contact.

## Avoid

- Copying Globant's identity, boilerplate, scale, partners, clients, executives, ticker, awards, contact, or AI positioning into another organization's release.
- Unsupported market leadership, uniqueness, savings, growth, customer, or performance claims.
- Inflated phrasing such as “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” or “world-class” without attributable evidence.
- Dense technical descriptions that obscure the news.
- Quotes that sound like product copy or contain facts the release cannot verify.

## Adapt by organization

Infer no default voice from the examples. Apply the announcing organization's terminology, brand voice, geography, capitalization, approved descriptions, and risk requirements. A startup, nonprofit, public company, government body, university, and global enterprise require different levels of context and review.
