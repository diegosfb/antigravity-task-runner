# Bench matching

Apply constraints in this order:
1. Availability and allocation compatibility.
2. Mandatory skill/profile requirements.
3. Mandatory geo/timezone/language requirements.
4. Seniority requirement or permitted adjacent band.
5. Relevant secondary skills/domain experience.
6. Start-date fit.

Classify matches:
- Strong: meets mandatory constraints and core skill/seniority expectations.
- Conditional: plausible with a clearly identified gap, training, timing, or location exception.
- Weak: substantial mismatch; do not count as filled.

For conditional matches, estimate enablement needed and keep recruiting contingency when the match is uncertain.
