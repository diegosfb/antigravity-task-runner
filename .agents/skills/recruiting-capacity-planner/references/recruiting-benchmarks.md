# Configurable recruiting benchmarks

These are starter assumptions only. Replace with observed company data as soon as available.

## Stage durations: expected working/calendar ranges
- campaign setup / calibration: 2-5 business days
- sourcing to first qualified slate: 3-10 business days
- screening: 2-5 business days
- technical/functional interviews: 5-10 business days
- client/final interview when required: 3-10 business days
- offer preparation/approval: 2-5 business days
- candidate decision: 2-7 calendar days
- notice period: 0-30+ calendar days depending on market and candidate status
- onboarding/access: 2-10 business days
- ramp to normal productivity: 1-4 weeks depending on project complexity

## Starter funnel conversions
Use only when no actual funnel data exists and label clearly as assumptions.
- sourced prospect -> screened/qualified: 15-30%
- qualified -> interview process: 50-75%
- interview process -> final/offer-ready: 25-50%
- offer -> accepted: 65-85%
- accepted -> started: 90-98%

Do not multiply midpoint percentages blindly for every campaign. Adjust for scarcity, seniority, geo, compensation competitiveness, interview burden, brand/channel strength, and mandatory constraints.

## Complexity modifiers
Increase time/risk for niche skills, lead/principal roles, mandatory on-site requirements, small talent markets, unusual combinations of skills, client interviews, or large simultaneous ramps. Reduce time when qualified bench, warm pipelines, reusable campaigns, or validated high-performing channels exist.

## Replacement
When historical data is supplied, calculate benchmarks by geo x skill family x seniority where sample size supports it; otherwise back off to broader cohorts and show sample-size limitations.
